## History

- **The daily chat eval only logged its result** (decision changed 2026-11-27, wi_94ad6bab). Before: a daily run below its bar, or one that threw, ended in a log line (`chat eval run finished` or `chat eval run failed`) and nothing in Needs you; on 2026-11-27 the 00:00Z run passed 0 of 12 and chat stayed broken for about 15 hours unseen. Replaced by the `chat_eval_failed` Needs-you alert of [04-chat-agent](../04-chat-agent.md); the bar and the eval questions are unchanged.
- **`chat.send` took `session_id` and `text` only** (decision changed 2026-11-27, spec chat-images, the images decision at the top of 04-chat-agent.md). Before: the input was `session_id` (optional) and `text`; the model got only text, and the chat stored no image.
- **Every provider answer that did not end was `model_error`** (decision changed 2026-11-27). Before: a refused, cut-off or failed provider answer all failed the turn as `model_error`, shown as "The model did not finish its answer.", and no text of the provider's came back, so a provider that refused tools without calling the model (`claude-cli`, the repo's setting) looked like a model fault on every turn and the daily eval logged 0/12 with no reason. Replaced by `model_refused`, `model_cut_off` and `model_error` with `detail` in [04-chat-agent](../04-chat-agent.md), and by the claude-cli provider carrying tools (docs/plugins/llm-provider.md).
- **Not built: running the eval suite here** (decision changed 2026-11-27, local-task-tk_eea9af6d phase 3 of 3). Before: the eval questions were checked in and only their shape was tested; nothing sent them to the chat, judged a reply or held a pass bar. Replaced by the daily eval run and the pass bar of 8 of [04-chat-agent](../04-chat-agent.md).
- **No HTTP route, capability or dashboard page called the chat turn** (decision changed 2026-11-26, local-task-tk_eea9af6d phase 2, phase 1 of 2). Before: `chatTurn` ran only from tests; nothing reached it, and the store kept no turn's outcome or reason, only its messages and spend. Replaced by the `chat.send`, `chat.transcript` and `chat.turns` capabilities and the `controller_chat_turns` row of [04-chat-agent](../04-chat-agent.md).
