## History

- **The Calendar showed only calendar and local events, with no filter** (until 2026-11-28, the agent entries decision at the top of 09-dashboard-tasks-calendar.md, phase 1 of local-task-tk_00ebf1cf). Before: schedules, their runs and next slots were not on the page, and the toolbar had only the Week / Month switch.
- **The New event dialog only saved here** (until 2026-11-25, the "Also add to Google Calendar" decision at the top of 09-dashboard-tasks-calendar.md, phase 3 of local-task-tk_dc8c579a). Before: the dialog had no copy option and showed no copy error; an event stayed local.
- **The Calendar page was read-only, and with no calendar connected it showed an empty state instead of the grid** (until 2026-11-25, the New event decision at the top of 09-dashboard-tasks-calendar.md, phase 2 of local-task-tk_dc8c579a). Before: no way to add, edit or delete an event on the page, and local events stayed hidden with no calendar connected.
- **The Popover's body was `flex-1`, and a Popover with no room beside its anchor was only clamped under it** (decision changed 2026-11-24, the Popover sizing decision in 09-dashboard-tasks-calendar.md, owner #92). Before: the body grew from a zero basis, so WebKit drew it 24px tall under a scrollbar; and under a wide chip near the window's bottom the panel was pushed up over the chip instead of flipping above it.
- **Chips with a colour strip, month cells that grew, empty cells outside the month, a strip-only month on a phone** (until 2026-11-24; replaced by the macOS Calendar decision at the top of 09-dashboard-tasks-calendar.md, owner #92 and #75). Before: every event chip (all day or timed) started with a 4px colour strip, Week chips sat on `surface-2` with "all day" as the time; Month cells had a minimum height and grew with their events, listed 3 events then a plain "+N more" line that did nothing, put the number at the top left, drew the days before and after the month as empty cells (the read asked only for the month), and below `sm` showed each event as a bar across the cell with no way to read it but pressing it.
- **The event popover was as tall as the window and its link ended the body** (until 2026-11-24; replaced by the popover placement decision at the top of 09-dashboard-tasks-calendar.md). Before: long details made the panel fill the window, so a chip low in the window opened it at the window's top, and "Open in Google Calendar" scrolled out of view at the body's end.
- **An event chip showed its whole text only in a tooltip** (until 2026-11-24, the event details decision at the top of 09-dashboard-tasks-calendar.md, owner request #91). Before: a Week chip cut its title with `Clamp` (with its own "Show more") and a Month line with an ellipsis, the whole time, title and source were only in the chip's `title` tooltip, and the read answered no place, notes, people or links (the sync stored only the title and times).
- **Calendar: text buttons, a sideways-scrolling week of bordered event cards, today outlined in accent** (decision 18 changed 2026-11-24, owner feedback "still ugly calendar design"). Before: the toolbar was the Week/Month switch, then Previous, Next and Today as text buttons and Sync as a bordered text button, with the period as an h2 on the line; Week was seven columns with a 56rem minimum width that scrolled sideways inside the card at every width; each day's events were a bordered list of cards (title, then time) inside the card; today's heading was circled with a 2px `accent` outline; Month was the shared `Table` (one card per row below `sm`). Now see the current decision.
- **The Calendar week was seven stacked rows and the controls two rows** (decision 18 changed 2026-11-20, the Calendar page decision). Before: Week was an `ol` of seven `li` rows, one per day, "so it reads on a phone", and the Week/Month switch and the period buttons sat in two rows above the card.
- **The Calendar page had Day and Upcoming** (until 2026-11-15, the Calendar page decision in 09-dashboard-tasks-calendar.md (once under the Tasks page decision in 09-dashboard-tasks.md); decision changed: "a Day / Upcoming control over today through 7 days ahead from `GET /api/v1/tasks`"). Before: a `SegmentedControl` Day / Upcoming, then the events of today (Day) or of each of today through 7 days ahead (Upcoming) from `GET /api/v1/tasks` with no project filter, with no new route; with a calendar and no events the empty state said "No events today." or "No events from today through the next 7 days." with no link. No other period could be shown, because that read places only today and the next 7 days.
- **Calendar: period title in the Card, "Not synced" and "No events" per day, source on every event, today unmarked** (decision 18 changed 2026-11-20, owner feedback). Before: the period ("Nov 9 – 15, 2026") was the title of the Card below the toolbar; each unsynced day printed "Not synced", each empty synced day "No events", a period with no synced day showed one note; every event row printed its source; today had no mark and events had no colour. Now: the heading is the toolbar's h2, the Card has no title, today is circled, events have a colour strip, the source shows only with several calendars, unsynced days are shaded with one note naming them, and empty days show no text.
