## History

- **Nothing calls the scheduler and no action is defined** (decision 2026-11-22, wi_a519d81a, phase 1 of 2; replaced 2026-11-23, wi_9a35f49052e147d9bd48df8ecb40eb94). Before: "Nothing calls the scheduler yet and no action is defined: this phase is the records, the cadences and the claim." `runDueSchedules` had no caller, and a fired occurrence ran nothing.
- **Skipping was still to come, as "only when the factory has no open work"** (decision 2026-11-23, wi_9a35f49052e147d9bd48df8ecb40eb94, phase 2 of 2; replaced 2026-11-23, wi_c9777390927f4e03a99cd7f292d1b9fb). Before: "Still to come. The per-schedule option 'only when the factory has no open work' with its `skipped` outcome". Every due occurrence fired whatever the queue held, and no helper tied the feedback run's sandbox start and stop to the agent run. Now a `skip_when_busy` schedule skips at 15 or more queued implement runs, and the feedback run starts and stops its own sandbox.
- **No action plugin existed** (decision 2026-11-23, wi_9a35f49052e147d9bd48df8ecb40eb94, phase 2 of 2; replaced 2026-11-23, wi_af086ea57ce54b0b8f9297f88bfaa146). Before: "No action plugin exists yet: the owner has not named one." A schedule had no action kind, a run row kept no output or cost, and no token cap held an occurrence. Now the `routed` plugin runs a build, agent or feedback action per schedule, and the claim pass holds a model-calling occurrence over the schedule's 200000-token cap at a checkpoint that the common `/approve` and `/reject` answer.
