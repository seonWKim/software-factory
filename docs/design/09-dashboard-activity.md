# 9. Status page: dashboard Activity page

- **The Activity page lists every autonomous actor and its recent actions, from one capability (decision added 2026-11-26, phase 2 of 3 of local-task-tk_9a6d93d9; current; extends the Running now decision below, replaces none).** Layout (owner answer 1): `PageHeader` Activity; a `Card` Actors holding a `DataTable` of one row per actor (name in plain words, a `Badge` for its state, what it does now, last and next run, and a `Button` Transcript); a `Card` About <actor> for the selected row with a `KeyValue` of now, last and next run and a `DataTable` of its recent actions (outcome, reason, a Transcript link or the log line). The read is the `activity.actors` capability (`src/api/capabilities/activity.ts`, `GET /api/v1/activity`, listed in `src/api/index.ts`); its query is `readActivity` in `src/ui/activity-api.ts`, called only by the capability, and the page (`web/src/views/activity/page.tsx`, through `web/src/api/activity.ts` and the generated client) and the chat call that capability, with no hand-written route. Actors are the `BACKGROUND_JOBS` of `src/controller/background-jobs.ts`; each has its words (`ACTOR_WORDS`), and `tests/design-system.test.ts` fails for a registered job without them. Its actions are the `controller_activity` rows of the actor, newest first, read per actor by its job's kind (at most 10), so a busy actor never pushes a quieter one's rows out of the read; the read returns none older than 30 days (owner answer 2, `ACTIVITY_RETENTION_DAYS`), whether or not the retention pass has run. An open factory alert shows in a Card above Actors with a Clear button that dismisses it (owner answer 3, the alarm dismissal of [05-alarms.md](05-alarms.md)). The page's text is in `web/src/i18n/en/activity.ts` under the `actors.` prefix (`activity.` belongs to Work's section, `en/work.ts`). The page component is `ActivityActorsPage`, exported from `web/src/views/pages.tsx`; its route and sidebar entry are not added yet (`web/src/App.tsx` and `web/src/pages.ts`, which still redirect `/app/activity` to Work).

- **Running now is drawn on Work's Activity section only (decision changed 2026-11-22, wi_149faba7; current; replaces Running now on both Work and the Runners page, see History).** `ActivityPage` (`web/src/views/pages.tsx`) is the one place the Running now table shows; Health's Runners tab has the worker processes only ([09-health.md](09-health.md)). The "Runs failed" figure links to `/app/health?tab=failures&window=24h`. Work's `PageHeader` reads "Work = what the factory is doing".

- **Work and Activity sit under System in the sidebar (decision changed 2026-11-15; current; replaces "Work and Activity are in the Factory group", from the menu-groups decision of 2026-11-11, see History).** `PAGES` in `web/src/pages.ts` gives Work (`/app/items`) and Activity (`/app/activity`) group `system`, as the first two System entries, before Health: System reads Work, Activity, Health, Failures, Spend, Rules, Runners, Data. The Factory group is gone with the Needs you page (the 2026-11-15 landing decision above). Paths, redirects, page content and `web/src/App.tsx` are unchanged.
