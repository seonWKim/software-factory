# 9. Status page: dashboard Rules page, waiting rows and verdicts

- **A waiting row is one compact two-line row: title with Promote and ⋯ at its right, then a truthful state and a one-line gist (decision changed 2026-11-27, owner "improve the UX and UI of the Rules section"; current; replaces the "Running" pill of an open proposal, Promote on its own line, the "<kind>: N files" line, the group counts and the always-shown group lines, see [History](history/09-dashboard-rules/2026-11-27-compact-waiting-rows.md)).**
  - **State.** A waiting row shows a pill only when it says something the section does not: `held` reads "Held" (`waitPill`, `rules-shared.tsx`). An open or pending entry and a proposed skill draft show none: they sit under Waiting for you, and nothing runs until the owner decides (an open proposal used to read "Running"). A decided row keeps "Judged: <decision>".
  - **Actions.** `ListRow` has an `action` slot: one small visible Button before the ⋯, level with the title's first line; below `sm` it takes a line of its own under the row's content, the ⋯ staying top right. Promote (secondary: the page keeps one accent button) is the row's action; the ⋯ menu holds Hold and Reject (danger), then Copy ID. The same for skill drafts (Promote; Hold and Reject in ⋯) and standing proposals (Accept; Decline in ⋯). Every verdict still opens its dialog, so no row grows (C-050).
  - **Title.** A waiting row's title (rule and skill rows) is its whole text, wrapped at every width (`WRAP_TITLE`: `break-keep`, `wrap-anywhere` for an unbroken identifier), never cut to one line; it is plain text, so the rule's ID moved from the title's tooltip to the ⋯ menu's Copy ID (every rule row has the ⋯, a decided or read-only one with Copy ID alone).
  - **Gist.** Up to two lines under the title, then an ellipsis (full text and the full target path in its tooltip). A proposal's: its change in plain words with the file name, "Changes review.md", "Turns a learning into a rule in SKILL.md", "Adds learnings.md" (several files: "<first> and N more"; none: "Names no file to change"), then ": " and its gist. `GET /api/v1/rules` gives each waiting proposal `gist` (`src/ui/rules-preview.ts`, from the `show` it already reads for the excerpt, no new route): the first line it adds, else the first line of its prose change, description or root reason, list or heading marks dropped, at most 160 characters, through the Withholder; null when none. A candidate's: "Rule: " and its draft's first line, left out when it only repeats the title. The before and after lines stay behind "Why and where"; its What part reads the same change line and "Path: <target>".
  - **One count, help on demand.** The page header's "N waiting" badge is the one count: the Waiting for you card and its groups (Turn on auto-approve, Skill changes, Rule candidates, Structure proposals) show no count beside their titles (with the ledger off, the skill card keeps its count, there is no header badge). The groups' lines on what promoting does sit in one closed "How promoting works" Disclosure at the end of the card, one line per group that has rows.
  - **Why:** the owner read "Running" on entries nothing was running, each row was four lines tall with Promote on a line of its own, and what a proposal changes was hidden behind a disclosure (2026-11-27).
- **Skill drafts wait on the Rules page and take Promote, Hold and Reject there and from the CLI, through one function (decision changed 2026-11-24, local-task-tk_90fd7e80 phase 3; current; replaces "the Rules page's verdicts take ledger IDs only", see History).**
  - **Read.** `GET /api/v1/proposals` (`src/ui/improve-api.ts`) adds `skills`, the configured project's skill drafts newest first (`listSkillDrafts`, at most 100), beside the entity proposals: each draft's `id`, `state`, `reason` (a tried or held draft's, else null), `skill_ref` (the skill path), `why` (the check names it was drafted against, `; `-separated), `before` and `after` (the pass rate now and with the draft, from its baseline and scratch runs), `created_at` and `verdicts`: promote, hold and reject for a proposed draft, promote and reject for a held one, none otherwise. The skill path and the checks leave through the Withholder; the draft text and the old text never leave.
  - **Rows.** Waiting for you has a "Skill changes" section (`web/src/views/rules-skills.tsx`) before the candidates, one `ListRow` per proposed or held draft: "Change the skill <path>", its state as a `StatusBadge` (proposed as a pending rule, held as held), when it was drafted, the failed checks and "Tests passed: <now> now, <with> with the change". The owner gets Promote as a visible button and the draft's verdicts in one `OverflowMenu`; Promote and Reject ask first in the shared `Dialog` (Promote says it writes the change, re-runs the skill's tests and can take minutes), Hold acts at once. The waiting count includes them. With the ledger off the same rows sit in their own Waiting for you card. No new component.
  - **Write.** `POST /api/v1/rules/skill-verdict` with `{ id, verdict }`, one of the Rules page's write routes (`RULE_WRITES`), behind server.ts's guards and the token the page has only when its user is the owner. `writeSkillVerdict` checks the body (400), then the owner (`ownerOf`, as the rule verdicts: 403, nothing written), then that the draft is the configured project's (404), then calls the core: hold and reject change the state; promote writes SKILL.md and re-runs the suite before it answers. A refusal of the core (a draft in another state, a skill outside `capabilities.skill_folders`) is a 409 with the core's reason and nothing written; so is Promote with no skill wiring.
  - **CLI.** `factory improve skill <id> promote|hold|reject` builds the dashboard's parts (`buildUiParts`) and runs the same `writeSkillVerdict`: it prints the new state, or the refusal and exits 1.
  - **Wiring.** `buildUiParts` builds the skill wiring (`skillImproveWiring` over the UI's sandbox, runner and model; none without `capabilities.skill_folders`, a model or a runner) and hands the UI deps `skillImprove`: the core's three verdict functions and that wiring, passed in by the composition root as the eval functions are, so no route imports the core.
  - **Why:** the improve run stored skill drafts that nothing could decide; the owner decides them where they decide rules, and the CLI gives the same result.
- **A promoted prose proposal gets a task to build it; card and dialog share one reading order (decision changed 2026-11-20, local-task-tk_3b38699c, "Promote proposals that need building"; current; adds a task to the prose decision below, replaces the 2026-11-16 detail order, see History).**
  - **Build task.** After the prose promote's verdict (`promoteProposal` in `src/ui/rules-promote.ts`) the route stores one plain task: ID `tk_` + the proposal ID in lower case (`proposalTaskId`; an ID not `P-` plus letters, digits, `-`, `_` is a 400 before any CLI), the proposal's title (from `pending`, read before the verdict), and a body of `Rationale`, `Change`, `Target`, `Evidence`, `Proposal` lines through the Withholder (design 02-tasks-build). A stored task is a retry: 200, nothing sent; a promote already in the verdict log (`TacitLedger.decided`) is not resent; a refused verdict stores no task. The answer adds `task_id`; the task is not built, in no project. `GET /api/v1/rules` gives each proposal's `task` (null before); the card links "Task to build it: <task ID>" to Tasks.
  - **Reading order.** `ReadingOrder` (`web/src/views/rules-detail.tsx`), card and dialog: 1 What to promote (description, at most two lines; prose change; a class's rule and exception; kind; target), 2 Why (count, the root reason's first sentence), 3 Examples (ID, date, one line: quote, else proposal, else root reason; 3 shown, then "Show all N"), 4 Status of what it cites (`cited` in the entry read: each evidence ID's newest ledger verdict), then Where and Related; empty parts left out. The whole rationale and Now/Proposed lines sit in one closed Disclosure, "Full rationale and lines".
  - **Why:** a change to build left nothing to build it from, and the detail had no fixed order. Tests: `tests/rules-page.test.ts`, `tests/ui-rules-*.test.ts*`.
- **A proposal shows its prose change and is promoted as applied to its target (decision changed 2026-11-22, wi_92984ac4, "Rules: proposal details and full incident IDs"; current; replaces "Promote of a proposal is off until its Now and Proposed lines are read" from the 2026-11-18 apply decision below, see History).** `evolve show <P-id>` now prints JSON; the parser reads it first and falls back to the text form (docs/plugins/tacit-ledger.md). The detail shows `rationale` under Why (as the root reason), `change` (free text) under What it would change, and each `evidence` ID as an evidence row with its ID only (the JSON gives no date or quote); the incident count is the number of IDs. `GET /api/v1/rules/entry` adds `change`, through the Withholder. **Promote:** a proposal with Now and Proposed lines applies them as before; one with neither has Send on once a reason is given, says "Promoting records the verdict and creates a task to build it." (`rules.consequencePromoteBuild`), and the plugin runs only `evolve verdict <P-id> promote --reason <r> --applied-to <target>` and writes no file. A proposal whose row names no target has Promote, the button and its ⋯ item, off, with "Promote is off: this proposal names no file it changes." on the row as the draft-error line does; no dialog opens. The route's body is unchanged. Incident IDs keep their hyphens (`T-20261017-de7e`) in the `근거` line, draft lines and the related-rule match. **Not built:** a backfill of IDs already cut in the rules file; dates or quotes for JSON evidence. **Why:** proposals showed only their kind and Promote was always off.
- **A waiting row shows what it changes and has one ⋯ menu (decision changed 2026-11-20, wi_318a75cf, "Rules page: decide a proposal at a glance"; current except two parts replaced 2026-11-22 by the two-section decision in 09-dashboard-rules.md: the excerpt is behind "Why and where" and Promote is a visible button; replaces the row's collapsed-only before and after and its three verdict buttons, see History).** A waiting proposal's row shows, with no click, its change line ("<kind>: <short file name>", the target's last path segment, so "bin/evolve" reads "evolve"; the full target stays in "Why and where") and a Before and After excerpt in `ValueBlock`s. `GET /api/v1/rules` adds `excerpts`, a map from a waiting proposal's ID to `{ before?, after? }`, read through `TacitLedger.show` once per waiting proposal on each load (local, no network): the first changed lines of its current and proposed text (the lines both share at the start and end are dropped), at most 3 lines and 200 characters a side, each through the Withholder. A proposal whose ledger gives neither side has no key, and the row shows no excerpt line and no "not given"; a failed `show` is the same. Promote, Hold and Reject are the items of one `OverflowMenu` (⋯) on the row of a class or a proposal, Promote first and Reject last in the danger tone; they open the same dialogs, and closing one returns focus to the ⋯ button. A disabled Promote keeps its reason in the row (the draft-error line). A judged entry, or a viewer who is not the owner, gets no menu.
- **Rules: Promote of a proposal applies it after a diff and a confirm; the agents home is picked from the known homes; no copy names the config file or a command (decision changed 2026-11-18, phase 4 of local-0ffe3059-d863-4717-b3eb-1c72a06e444e; current; replaces "the owner makes the change by hand" and "put the CLIs in `<home>/bin`", see History; its "Send stays off until both lines are read" replaced 2026-11-22 by the prose decision above).** The promote dialog shows the proposal's Now and Proposed lines (its detail's Before → after) and sends only the reason (a proposal with neither: the prose decision above). The plugin replaces the Now lines in the target only when they occur there once, then runs `evolve verdict … --applied-to <target>` (`evolve` has no apply subcommand; docs/plugins/tacit-ledger.md); a refusal, a non-zero exit or the timeout leaves the file as it was and the dialog shows the error. With the ledger off, `home.homes` lists the known homes (`AGENTS_HOME` when set, `~/.agents`) as radios with the chosen one's check (both CLIs executable, else the missing file); choosing stores nothing and `integrations` stays the plugin name. `en.ts` has no "factory.config.json", "npm run", "factory pack" or "by hand" outside `data.` and `runners.` keys (`tests/web-user-copy.test.ts`).
- **The Rules page's verdict dialog and expanded row show the evidence behind an entry (decision changed 2026-11-16; current; its detail order replaced 2026-11-20 by the reading order decision at the top; full text in History).** The Promote, Hold and Reject dialogs show, above their inputs, the entry's full title (wrapped, never cut) and then its detail, as the card's "Why and where", read-only. Every new text passes the Withholder in `readRuleEntry`. Long Korean titles and quotes wrap inside the dialog; `npm run check:responsive` checks the page at merge. **Why:** the owner could not see what an entry was based on.
- **Promote of a class encodes the rule (decision changed 2026-11-13; current; replaces "promote sends --rule only"; full text in History).** `POST /api/v1/rules/verdict` takes `text` (the wording), `default` and `exception` on promote of a class, each required and 1 to 2000 characters, and refuses them on anything else (400). The `agents-home` plugin checks the file and reads the draft (409, nothing written), writes the block, sends `tacit verdict … --encoded-to` (file put back on a failure) and runs `sync-agents` (docs/plugins/tacit-ledger.md). `GET /api/v1/rules` gives each entry `judged`, `draft` and `draft_error`. **Why:** the owner's ledger refuses a promote without `--encoded-to`, so the owner did every promotion by hand.
- **The Rules page shows what each entry changes and why, in two groups (decision changed 2026-11-13; current; replaces "cards show state, ID and title only", see History).** The decision that changed: **a card answers what it would change, why and where, and candidates and proposals are separate groups.** **Groups:** rule candidates (`C-`) first, then structure proposals (`P-`), each a `Section` with its count and one line on what promoting one does: a candidate's rule reaches every run's prompt from the next run on; a proposal's promote records the verdict only and the owner applies the change by hand (the owner's answer). An empty group is not shown. **Card:** state, ID, title, and one line of what it would change: for a candidate "Rule: <title>" (the promote dialog's default wording; `evolve pending` lists no draft), for a proposal "<kind>: <target>" from `evolve pending`. **Detail on demand:** "Why and where" (`aria-expanded`) reads `GET /api/v1/rules/entry?id=` (`readRuleEntry` in `src/ui/rules-api.ts`) once when the card opens, never on page load and never polled. The route calls `TacitLedger.show(id)` only (`evolve show <id>`, docs/plugins/tacit-ledger.md), passes every text through the Withholder, and returns `{ id, detail, error }`: 400 for an ID that is neither `C-` nor `P-` before the ledger runs; `detail` null when the ledger is off, has no `show` or prints nothing; `error` a failed read. The page shows, in this order, What it would change (the draft rule and its exceptions, or the kind and target), Why (how many times it came up, the root reason) and Where it applies (the scope); the owner's quoted words, Before → after and Related, and leaving out an empty Why or Where, changed 2026-11-16 (the evidence decision above). A detail that is null or failed reads "The detail could not be read." and the card keeps its title and buttons. A viewer who is not the owner reads the detail too, read-only. **Text:** the group lines, labels and the unread line are `t()` keys in `web/src/i18n/en.ts`. The verdict dialogs, owner check and request guards are unchanged; nothing new writes. **Why:** the owner could not judge an entry from the page alone (2026-11-13: the section was hard to read and understand).
