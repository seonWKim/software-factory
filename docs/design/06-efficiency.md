# 6. Measurement: efficiency

- **Health shows how fast the factory finishes items and what each finished item takes (new decision, 2026-11-20, wi_d8011dd9, "Health: an Efficiency tab for build speed"; current).** Why: Health showed checks, pauses, sleeps, merges, Spend and Failures, but nothing showed how much the factory finishes or what a finished item takes, so there was no way to tell whether a change to the factory makes it faster or cheaper. The data was already stored; this adds no table, column, migration, backfill or write path.
  - **Finished.** A factory item (`origin` `factory`; adopted PRs are left out, owner's answer) that is `CLOSED` with `close_reason` `completed` and has a stored merge evaluation (design 06-measurement). It counts at the evaluation's `merged_at`, never at the item's last update. An item with two merge evaluations counts once, at the latest.
  - **Left out.** `CLOSED` factory items whose `close_reason` is not `completed`, closed (by `updated_at`) in the 8 weeks shown: cancelled (`wont_do`, `duplicate`), rejected and failed. Shown as one line of numbers, never in the charts.
  - **Charts.** Finished items per hour (24 bars, the current hour last), per UTC day (14 bars, today last) and per ISO week (8 bars, Monday 00:00 UTC, the current week last), each with its total as text.
  - **Runs counted.** Every run of the item of the kinds Spend counts (`SPEND_KINDS`: triage, implement, review, resolve, update), retries included, once claimed or finished; a run never claimed did nothing and is skipped.
  - **Tokens per item.** The sum of `runTokenCount` over those runs, the same stored usage as Spend. A run has no stored usage when its outcome is null, or a succeeded outcome has neither `data.tokens` nor `data.usage`, or a failed one has no `usage`; this is read from the outcome (`runHasUsage`), never from `runTokenCount`'s 0. An item with such a run is left out of its week's token median and percentile and counted in that week's "not counted" number; the runs are also counted, over the 8 weeks, as one number. `runTokenCount` itself is unchanged.
  - **Time per item.** Total: `merged_at` minus `created_at`. Agent: the sum of the runs' `finished_at` minus `started_at` (`started_at` is the latest claim's). Waiting: total minus agent (queue, waiting on files, waiting on the owner). An item with a run missing `started_at` or `finished_at` is left out of its week's three time figures and counted in a "not counted" number.
  - **Figures.** Per ISO week of `merged_at`: median (the mean of the two middle values for an even count) and nearest-rank 90th percentile (`sorted[ceil(0.9 × n) − 1]`) of tokens, total, agent and waiting time. A week with no value shows "no data", never 0.
  - **Week-over-week change.** Each weekly figure (count, tokens, times) shows its change from the week before as a signed whole percent with a U+2212 minus (`−18%`, `+5%`); none when the week before has no figure or it is 0. The oldest week shown compares with the week before it (read too). The running week's change is shown, marked "so far" (owner's answer).
  - **Slowest and costliest.** The picked week's 5 items with the largest total time, and its 5 with the highest USD cost (owner's answer: USD, as Spend; the sum of the runs' known `outcomeCost`, items with no known cost not listed), each its title linking to its item page. The picked week is the latest by default; a click on a weekly bar picks it; the pick stays in the browser, not the URL.
  - **Route.** `GET /api/v1/efficiency` (`src/ui/efficiency.ts`, wired in `src/ui/api.ts`), read-only (a POST gets 405 like the other read routes). The counting is pure in `src/core/efficiency.ts` (`summarizeEfficiency`); `readEfficiency` reads through existing store methods only: `listMergeEvaluations`, `getWorkItem` and `listRuns` per merged item, and one `listWorkItemsWhere` of closed items (at most 10,000). Titles pass the Withholder.
  - **Page.** The Efficiency tab on Health (`?tab=efficiency`, after Merged without review, before GitHub budget; design 09-status-page), one `Card` with `CardSection`s, `EmptyState` and plain-element bars on token colours as in Spend; no chart dependency. Strings are `efficiency.*` keys in `web/src/i18n/en/health.ts`.
  - Not built: a CLI command, per-tier, per-project or per-kind breakdowns, alerts or budgets on these numbers, and a fix of `runTokenCount`'s 0 for other callers.
