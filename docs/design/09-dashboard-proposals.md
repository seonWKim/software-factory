# 9. Status page: dashboard Proposals page

- **A Proposals page lists the improve run's entity proposals with their evidence and takes Promote, Hold and Reject (new decision, 2026-11-21, issue #496; current).** The proposals themselves, their store and the filing on promote are in 07-improve-report.md; their settings in 07-self-improvement.md.
  - **Where.** `/app/proposals` (`web/src/views/proposals.tsx`), a routable page with no sidebar link yet, like Runners and Data (`group: "hidden"` in `web/src/pages.ts`). Why: the sidebar's links are pinned by the shell tests, which this change could not edit; a sidebar link (or a link from the Rules page's improve report) is a follow-up.
  - **Read.** `GET /api/v1/proposals` (`src/ui/improve-api.ts`, beside Run now, served in `src/ui/server.ts` beside the Rules reads): the configured project's newest 100 proposals, newest first. Each carries its state, skip reason, error code, run count, file, the model's reason, the diff, the before and after evidence (each case's verdict, catch and false-alarm rates), a withheld one's rules, whether its change needs a human merge, its verdict time and whether its item was filed. Free text (error code, reason, diff) leaves through the Withholder on `run_outcome`. `csrf_token` is set only when the page's factory user passes `approve_spec`. The same answer carries `skills`, the improve run's skill drafts, which the Rules page shows and decides (09-dashboard-rules-waiting.md, since 2026-11-24); this page lists the entity proposals only.
  - **Page.** One `PageHeader` with a one-line description, then two `Card`s: "Waiting for you" (`pending` and `held`) and "Decided, watching and skipped". Each proposal is a `ListRow` in a bordered list (as the Rules page): a plain title ("Change prompts/review.md after "<failure in words>" failed <n> times"), its state as a `Badge`, a compact time with the local time in its title; then the model's reason marked unchecked, the evidence as a `KeyValue` ("Problems caught: 0% before, 100% after", "False alarms", "Test cases"), and the diff behind "Show the change" (`Disclosure`, `ValueBlock`), since a diff is long. A watching, skipped or withheld proposal says why in one line and offers no verdict.
  - **Verdict (decision changed 2026-11-22, wi_60473dff; current; replaces the three-button row with no confirm dialog, see History).** A waiting proposal's row shows Promote (`secondary`, small: one per row, so not the page's primary) and has Promote, Hold and Reject (danger) in its `OverflowMenu`, as patterns.md Actions asks for three or more actions on a row. Hold applies only to a `pending` proposal: a held one does not show it. Promote and Reject cannot be undone (no verdict leaves `promoted` or `rejected`, and promote files a work item), so each asks first in the shared `Dialog`: the title names the action and the proposal, one line says what confirming does and that it cannot be undone, and the confirm button repeats the action (Reject in the danger colour); a refused send shows in the dialog, which stays open. Hold acts at once, since a held proposal can still be promoted or rejected. With no token, a line says only someone who may approve plans can decide. A verdict is `POST /api/v1/proposals/verdict` with `{ id, verdict }` and the CSRF token, behind server.ts's write guards (Host, Origin, Content-Type, token, body size, JSON), then `approve_spec` (403, nothing written), then the proposal's own project: approve_spec is asked on the configured project, so a proposal of another project in the same store answers 404 and nothing is written. An `unchanged` answer (decided already) shows on its own line under the row. The list reloads after each verdict; nothing on the page changes a proposal before one.
  - **Solo and enterprise.** The same page and route; the owner check is the configured auth plugin's `approve_spec`.
