# 9. Status page: dashboard Proposals page

- **A Proposals page lists the improve run's entity proposals with their evidence and takes Promote, Hold and Reject (new decision, 2026-11-21, issue #496; current).** The proposals themselves, their store and the filing on promote are in 07-improve-report.md; their settings in 07-self-improvement.md.
  - **Where.** `/app/proposals` (`web/src/views/proposals.tsx`), a routable page with no sidebar link yet, like Runners and Data (`group: "hidden"` in `web/src/pages.ts`). Why: the sidebar's links are pinned by the shell tests, which this change could not edit; a sidebar link (or a link from the Rules page's improve report) is a follow-up.
  - **Read.** `GET /api/v1/proposals` (`src/ui/proposals-api.ts`, served in `src/ui/server.ts` beside the Rules reads): the configured project's newest 100 proposals, newest first. Each carries its state, skip reason, error code, run count, file, the model's reason, the diff, the before and after evidence (each case's verdict, catch and false-alarm rates), a withheld one's rules, whether its change needs a human merge, its verdict time and whether its item was filed. Free text (error code, reason, diff) leaves through the Withholder on `run_outcome`. `csrf_token` is set only when the page's factory user passes `approve_spec`.
  - **Page.** One `PageHeader` with a one-line description, then two `Card`s: "Waiting for you" (`pending` and `held`) and "Decided, watching and skipped". Each proposal is a list row: a plain title ("Change prompts/review.md after "<failure in words>" failed <n> times"), its state as a `Badge`, a compact time with the local time in its title; then the model's reason marked unchecked, the evidence as a `KeyValue` ("Problems caught: 0% before, 100% after", "False alarms", "Test cases"), and the diff behind "Show the change" (`Disclosure`, `ValueBlock`), since a diff is long. A watching, skipped or withheld proposal says why in one line and offers no verdict.
  - **Verdict.** A waiting proposal has Promote (primary), Hold (secondary, off while held) and Reject (danger) in one `ButtonRow`, as the spec names three buttons; with no token, a line says only someone who may approve plans can decide. A press is `POST /api/v1/proposals/verdict` with `{ id, verdict }` and the CSRF token, behind server.ts's write guards (Host, Origin, Content-Type, token, body size, JSON) and then `approve_spec` (403, nothing written). No confirm dialog: Hold can be undone by a later verdict, and Promote and Reject only set a state and file a draft item, never a file change. An `unchanged` answer (decided already) or a refusal shows on its own line under the row. The list reloads after each press; nothing on the page changes a proposal before one.
  - **Solo and enterprise.** The same page and route; the owner check is the configured auth plugin's `approve_spec`.
